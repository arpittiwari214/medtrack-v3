# MedTrack — user guide

For patients and the people who look after them.

MedTrack is a reminder tool, not a medical device. It doesn't give medical
advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.

---

## Getting started

### Install the app (Android)
1. Open the MedTrack download link in **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Tap the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow installing from Chrome.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
4. Allow **notifications** and **alarms & reminders** when asked. Without them,
   reminders can't ring.

### Choose how to use it
- **Sign in** if you have an account. Your medicines are backed up and also
  appear on the website.
- **Use without an account**: everything stays on this phone. You can sign in
  later to back it up.
- **Create a new account** opens the MedTrack website. Sign up there, then come
  back to the app and sign in.

## Every day

### When a reminder rings
- Tap **Taken** on the notification to record the dose without opening the app.
- Tap **Snooze** to be reminded again in 10 minutes.
- Tap the notification itself to open MedTrack. If you turned on *Read
  reminders aloud*, it says which medicine to take.

### The Today screen
- The big card shows the **next dose**: its colour, photo, name and time.
- **Hear it**: reads the medicine out loud.
- **Mark as taken**: records the dose.
- **Snooze**: choose 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.
- **Skip**: for a dose you're deliberately not taking. MedTrack asks first, so
  a stray tap doesn't count.
- **Overdue**: if you took it but forgot to record it, you can still mark it
  taken, up to 24 hours later.
- The bar at the bottom shows today's doses:

  | Colour | Means |
  |---|---|
  | Green | Taken |
  | Red | Missed |
  | Light grey | Skipped |
  | Dark grey | Not yet |

  The number (for example **2/5**) counts doses **taken**.

## Medicines

- **Add a medicine:** take a photo of the strip, choose the pill colour and the
  strip colour, and set the times. You can record a short voice note, such as
  *"the white one for sugar"*.
- **Who takes it?:** one phone can track medicines for several people, such
  as you and a parent.
- **Running low:** enter how many you have, and MedTrack warns you before you
  run out.

## Insights

- Your **adherence**: the share of doses you took, over the last days.
- One bar per day: tall is good, a **red** bar means every dose that day was
  missed, and a flat grey tick means nothing was due.
- A rate for each medicine, so you can see which one is easiest to forget.

## Visits

Add your next check-up on the **website**, and the phone reminds you. The
Visits screen has a button that opens the website.

## Settings

- **Language:** same as phone, English, or हिन्दी.
- **Reminders on this phone:** checks that reminders will ring, and shows how
  far ahead they're set. On **Vivo or iQOO** phones, tap *Steps for vivo or
  iQOO* and follow them. These phones block reminders unless you allow them.
- **Read reminders aloud** when you open them.
- **Sync now:** sends your latest changes to your account straight away.
- **Caregivers:** opens the website page where you invite family.

## Caregivers

### Inviting someone (patient)
1. Settings → **Caregivers** (or on the website: Settings → *Manage
   caregivers*).
2. Tap **Invite**. Enter their email, who they are to you, and what they may
   see: medicines, whether you took them, appointments, alerts.
3. They get an email from MedTrack (it may be in their Spam folder).
4. When they accept, the status changes from **Invited** to **Active**.
5. **Withdraw access** stops their access immediately.

### Accepting an invitation (caregiver)
1. Open the email from MedTrack and tap **Review the invitation**.
2. Tap **Create an account** (or **Sign in**), using **the same email the
   invitation was sent to**.
3. You come back to the invitation. Tap **Accept invitation**.
4. **People I care for** now shows the person. You get an email if they miss an
   important dose.

The invitation link works for 14 days.

## Your account

- **Forgot password?** On the sign-in screen. You get an email with a link
  that works for 1 hour.
- **Change password, sign out of all devices:** website → Settings.
- **Download your data** or **delete your account:** website → Settings.
  Deletion waits 14 days, so you can change your mind.

## Updates

- From version 4, MedTrack downloads small updates by itself. Close and reopen
  the app to see them.
- For bigger updates you'll see **"A new version of MedTrack is ready"**. Tap
  **Update now**, then **Update** when the phone asks.
- **Never uninstall MedTrack to update it.** If you use it without an account,
  uninstalling deletes your medicines and history.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The reminder didn't ring. | Settings → *Reminders on this phone*, and follow the steps it shows. Check that the phone isn't in a battery-saving mode that stops apps. |
| The app is slow to load the first time. | The server wakes up after a quiet spell, which can take up to a minute. Your medicines and reminders still work offline. |
| I didn't get the email. | Check Spam and Promotions. It comes from **MedTrack**, and the address ends in *brevosend.com*. |
| The invitation says it was sent to a different email. | Sign in (or create the account) with the exact address the invitation went to. |
| Does MedTrack tell me what dose to take? | No. It reminds you of what your doctor prescribed. It doesn't give medical advice. |
